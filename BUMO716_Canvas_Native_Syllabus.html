Course Description

Foundations of Consulting is designed to help students develop the three essential skills for a successful consulting career: teamwork, analysis, and presentation skills. Students will develop these skills through class discussion, individual exercises, and applied team exercises with a real client.

Course Objectives

Work constructively as a team to analyze a business situation and present clear, convincing, and well-supported recommendations. Approach problem-solving using a structured methodology.
Understand the business imperatives of consulting firms and the dynamics of the consulting industry. Reflect on your performance in a team and as an individual and make recommendations for future improvement.
What this course does not cover: This class will not teach data analytics, strategy, marketing, operations, finance, or every possible consulting framework. Students should build on the core curriculum and proactively seek outside knowledge using tools such as VBIC.

About the Instructors

Nicole M. Coomber

Professor Coomber completed her PhD in Education Policy and Leadership in 2012 at the University of Maryland. She is a Clinical Professor at the Robert H. Smith School of Business and Senior Assistant Dean for Experiential Learning. She teaches organizational behavior, management consulting, and leadership in undergraduate, MBA, and online MBA programs. Her consulting work includes HR engagements with Gilbane Associates, ABC Construction, Sequoia Equities, and LEARN Behavioral.

Albert “Ab” Krall ’81

Ab earned his undergraduate degree in Information Systems Management from Smith’s predecessor, the College of Business and Management. He spent 27 years with Accenture, working across industries before focusing on telecommunications clients including Verizon, AT&T, Iridium, BellSouth, and Vodafone. After retiring from Accenture, he served as a Managing Director at Deloitte until 2017. He has chaired the Smith School Advisory Board and continues to serve as a board member.
